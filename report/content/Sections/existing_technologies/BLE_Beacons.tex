\subsection{\gls{ble} Beacons}
\acrfull{ble} is a wireless communication technology design for low-power applications, operating in the 2.4GHz range. One application of \gls{ble} is \gls{ble} beacons, which are small wireless devices that periodically broadcast advertisement packets, containing identification information. These signals can be picked up by nearby devices, such as smartphones or dedicated receivers, without an established connection.

Unlike passive \gls{rfid} tags, conventional \gls{ble} beacons require an internal power source to broadcast the mentioned advertisement packets. This allows them to operate independently of a readers emitted electromagnetic field, similar to active \gls{rfid} tags. This allows their effective range to be substantially improved, depending on the environment and other factors.

Due to it's requirement for a power source, it's mostly used in indoor positioning, and similar static use-cases \cite{indoor-content-delivery,ble-occupancy-monitoring,ble-indoor-positioning}.
