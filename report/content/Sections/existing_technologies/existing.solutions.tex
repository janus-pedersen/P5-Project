\subsection{Existing solutions}
when looking for existing solutions on the market that contains and entire system, the market decreased. Many companies sell rfid tags and small readers, but this is very independent and relying on the consumer, rather than having a whole system with little setup and management on the consumer side. 

In regards to complete systems on the marked, we would like to highlight the following two companies. These companies were chosen based on their use of rfid tags in an independent system, with little to no mangement requirements on the consumer side. GPS tracker sells a fleet mangement system with GPS tracking for cars, vans, machinery and tolls. Furthermore, the product includes a fleet mangements platform in which you can access live-position, alarms, reports and history of rides. GPS tracker do not brand themselves on their tool-tracking, out of the four subscriptions packets, tool-tracking via rfid tags is only available in their two most expensive subscriptions. 

In terms of tracking all four subscriptions get access to an app and webplatform, in which consumers have access to a 12-month GPS history over routes and stops and a position. The frequentiality of which the positions updates is depending on the subscription package, ranging from around every 10 minutes to around every 10 seconds. 


The other highlighted company, GSM, have many solutions on the market. In regards to our project, the tool mangement bluetooth beacons and GSM blue tech will be looked into. On their webshop GSM sells a multitude of different bluetooth beacons, along with this they sell a subscription to GSM blue tech, which is a software that tracks the bluetooth beacons and is available on web, desktop and mobile phone. GSM sells beacons of many different sizes and lifespan ranging from 2 months til 8 years. These beacons are of BLE technology and use version 5.0.
