\subsection{RFID in use}

In the report \textit{A hardware-based RFID identification and tracking system for components in
digitalised construction logistics}\cite{Heuer2025}, Heuer, Jung and Brell-Cokcan present a hardware-based RFID system for automatically identifying and tracking building components on construction sites. Their motivation for this project was a report from German Lean Construction Institute, GLCI, from 2022, where they estimated that 33\% of the working time was spend on locating and moving material on a constuction site \cite{Przybilla2022}.
