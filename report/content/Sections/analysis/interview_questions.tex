\subsection{Interviewing potential users}

\begin{itemize}
    \item How many vans does your company have / use?
    \item How many tools, materials and alike does each van drive around with?
    \item How do you manage said tools, materials and alike?
    \item Do you loose/forget tools (how often)?
    \item How much time do you use looking for the correct tools or materials?
    \item How do you manage tools, that are not in every van?
    \item If you wanted to locate a specific tools, what is your current process?
    \item If you were to implement a system, how much use of tech would you want/not want?
    \item How much time would you be willing to spend configuring and maintaining an automated "smart" system?
\end{itemize}

with the goal of
\begin{itemize}
    \item Creating pie charts
    \item Understanding the needs of the potential users.
    \item Finding specific workflows and processes to automate
\end{itemize}
