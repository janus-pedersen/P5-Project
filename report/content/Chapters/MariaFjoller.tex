We want to develop an IoT system with a raspberry pi and an UHF RFID antenna, that can detect the presence of tools. assets and materials in vans. A separate GPS antenna would locate the van, and it would all feed into a website that shows each van, it's location, and the assets in their cargo space. 

We found existing reports from previous AAU students that have done a value chain analysis of a similar problem and solution. Their practical implementation differs from what we want to implement, but they still recognize that the problem is relevant and is the cause for substancial costs for an organization.
